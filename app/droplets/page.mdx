## Sound
While creating this site I ran into the predicament of having far too many resources on sound to make it useable, and wasn't too comfortable bringing my work identity into my sound community.
I decided to pull out all of the sound resources here to put them into their own home.

That home... Was Neocities! I love the idea of Neocities, it's a lot of fun and very addicting for a web dev who grew up in the early aughts of the internet with Geocities, Angelfire, etc.

As you can see from this screenshot, I __really__ went all-in with the nostalgia, coupled with the energetic chaos of today's growing culture of the Cozy Web/Yesterweb/Indieweb. There's a big call for a return to an _"uglier"_ web, a dip back into Web 1.0 and the jagged edges of the early internet. I already have plans for additional goofy sites to play around with, but this one was a TON of fun. I built it in a day and am stoked to go on personal "Code Jams" of my own with these.

I'm seriously excited to see what the young developers coming into their own have to show the world. I for one have done my part by making sure visitors meet BonziBuddy :)

## #A11y
> Accessibility tool that can help with multiple things. Your sites (in this particular case I just slapped this in there with Mastodon talk, but some of y'all probably have your own sites), art with text, anything where we're talking contrast and readability: https://color.a11y.com/
>
> It's not the only tool, but it's one I use. There are extensions for browsers too if it helps anyone with this!
>
> On accidentally being inaccessible: Unless you have a disability that pertains to a thing (in this case, low-vision and colorblindess as examples - these are not necessarily the only things that can be related here), you're not going to have the experience of someone who does.
>
> Feedback from people who actually have experience with examples like these is the only true way to know, so if you can get that and they have the headspace for it, it will be more helpful than any tool.
> 
> BUT THAT DOESN'T MEAN you shouldn't try to be accessible! And everyone's experiences are different. I am not an expert lol and I will make mistakes, but I can try to help send you the right way, if anyone ever needs it
